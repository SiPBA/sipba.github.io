Our contributions to IEEE NSS/MIC 2018

conferences
research
Published

November 20, 2018

The SiPBA research group presented 7 works at the 2018 IEEE Nuclear Science Symposium and Medical Imaging Conference in Sydney, Australia. The topics covered Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s Diseases, using a wide range of methodologies. Find here, ready to download, some of the posters that were presented at the conference:

  • Ensemble classification of heterogeneous biomarkers in the diagnosis of Parkinsonism
  • I[123]FP-CIT SPECT brain imaging for Parkinson’s diagnosis using contour lines
  • A Deep Decomposition of MRI to Explore Neurodegeneration in Alzheimer’s Disease
  • Improved separation of Alzheimer’s disease and related disorders using dual-point amyloid-PET
  • Segmentation of PET and SPECT Data Using Hidden Markov Random Fields in Order to Improve the Assisted Diagnosis of Neurodegenerative Diseases
  • Analysis of I[123]-Ioflupane SPECT intensity iso-surfaces to assist the diagnosis of Parkinsonism
