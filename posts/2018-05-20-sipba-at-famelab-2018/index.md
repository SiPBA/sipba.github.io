SiPBA member Francisco Jesus Martinez Murcia was able to classify to the spanish final of Famelab 2018, the [most prestigious scientific monologue contest worldwide](https://www.granadahoy.com/granada/investigadores-UGR-final-Famelab_0_1246675727.html). It ended with the victory of the Madrid-born Juan Margalef. The podium was completed by the Galician Ignacio Crespo and the Asturian Raquel Medialdea, in a gala presented by the comedian Ana Morgade, and attended by the king and queen of Spain.
